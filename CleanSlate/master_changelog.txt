CleanSlate beta 6.1
Number of noteworthy bugs and oversights fixed: 908



=== For players ===

= General improvements =

Simplified/prettified many tooltips
Fixed countless minor issues and oversights
Fixed various grammatical and spelling errors in localisation
Many opinion changes are shown to both characters involved (but only applied once, of course)
Many opinion modifiers are now applied to the correct character
Several opinions are now checked for the correct character
Improved game performance, dependent on enabled DLC, game rules, start date, etc. (your mileage may vary, naturally)
Added missing/corrected wrong notification events for lost/gained traits
Removed dlc requirements from event pictures



= Changes/oversights =

Open Succession is now only unlocked, not enacted when a religion is reformed with Agnatic/Enatic Clans, to be consistent with these features' tooltips
Only the 'feminist' Christian religions (Cathar, Messalian) start off with Agnatic-Cognatic gender succession, instead of all of them
All event-driven deaths occur instantly, rather than on clicking the event option
Various events check the number of personality traits, rather than overall number of traits, before possibly adding a personality trait
Events about healing from disease and wounds now instantly remove those traits in multiplayer, so you won't die from those traits before clicking the event option
Sons and brothers of rulers will only ask to join a holy order if in the court of a parent/sibling, since only they should have a say in disinheritance
A number of religious events can now be performed by antipopes as well as popes
The Fraticelli Papacy will now also use papal succession
Some Catholic-only crusade events expanded to include Fraticelli
Grace is now taken away from both claimant and attacker in third party CBs (other_claim and other_dejure_county_claim)
Murder plots can no longer be cancelled while in motion
Patched up trade post inaccuracies due to map changes, closely matching that of The Republic's original release
Restored the Contarini family in the Republic of Venice
Reminded historical republics that a trade post can be built in Amalfi
Most polygamy events opened up to characters of non-muslim religions with polygamy
Moved global flags from character history to title history, so they're set at the exact date, instead of on the chosen start date
Restricted Imperial Elective to Byzantine and Roman empires
You can now only select provinces with claimable title when using the Fabricate Claim action
Vassal mercenary leaders are no longer selectable as commanders, since they could be appointed to lead armies anyway
Extended Viking guidance to cover the southern half of France, as per history
Creating the HRE and restoring the Roman Empire now requires being feudal, since their special succession laws are clearly feudal only
Creating custom kingdoms and empires now makes your current capital the de jure capital of the newly created title
Allowed imperial vassals to change the succession laws on the kingdom title they created themselves, which only seems fair.
Removed DLC checks to ways to free tributaries, so that tributaries in title history can be removed through normal gameplay
The pope can now absolve tribal kinslaying too

Properly opened up merchant republics to women, rather than only through the loopholes and openings Paradox presumably was unaware of. Due to hardcoding issues, agnatic-cognatic and enatic-cognatic are not available. The minor title to override the seniority heir is now properly restricted to the family palace gender succession law.
- Merchant republic AI may now choose Equality and Enatic Clans when reforming a religion (they could already choose Harmonious).
- Decisions to become a merchant republic, whether as tribal, nomadic, or to become the Hansa, can now be picked by women, and can pick female vassals as patricians. Upon doing so, agnatic-cognatic and enatic-cognatic are changed to absolute cognatic if eligible to do so manually.
- Changing the gender succession law in the primary title of a patrician will now also set that law on the patrician's family palace, to ensure smooth successions.
- Added female cultural titles for female leaders of merchant republics
- Added female equivalent to 'Patriciate' government flavour
- Alternate start female patricians can now also start with cognatic

Unhardcoded various reformed pagan religion features:
 - Invasions and cheap raiding are distinctive features of Norse paganism, yet are part of its special religion feature, instead of being inherently part of the reformed religion. This increases the amount of freedom and meaningful choices that can be made in reforming a religion. The same should be possible with all other pagan reformed religions, hence the following:
   - Ritual Sacrifice (Bloodthirsty Gods) and Pyramids are no longer inherently reformed Aztec, have been added to the special Aztec feature, and are also available separately to Aztec pagan reformers
   - Animistic is no longer inherently reformed West African, has been added to the special West African feature, and is also available separately to West African pagan reformers
   - Haruspicy and Astrology are no longer inherently reformed Hellenic, have been added to the special Hellenic feature, and are also available separately to Hellenic pagan reformers
 - Additional checks have been added to make these religions' special features incompatible with other features to due redundancy and incompatibility.
 - Additional ai_will_do modifiers have been added to these individual features, analogous to adventuring and seafaring's modifiers for Norse pagans.
